Scrobbling options

Marking played or unplayed in Emby changes your Simkl history too. Playback is scrobbled either way.
Needs a Simkl Pro or VIP plan. Upgrade at simkl.com/vip.
  • Play, pause and stop are reported to Simkl as they happen and show on your profile as watching now; who can see that is set under Watching now privacy on Simkl.
  • An item is marked as watched when you stop after 80%; stopping earlier saves your position on Simkl, where the Playback Progress Manager lists it.
  • With manual marks on, marking a movie, an episode, a season or a whole series as played in Emby marks it as watched on Simkl too, and unmarking a single movie or episode also removes it from your Simkl history. Unmarking a whole season or series does not touch your Simkl history, by design, so a bulk change in Emby cannot wipe out your watch history.
  • With rewatches on, finishing something that is already in your Simkl history is logged as a rewatch; this needs a Simkl Pro or VIP account and is ignored otherwise.

Import history

Marks movies and episodes in your Emby library as played when they are watched on Simkl, with the date you watched them. Nothing is sent from Emby to Simkl.
Emby asks Simkl whether you watched anything new and marks only those items. The very first import goes through your whole history.

Start over

Goes through your whole Simkl history again, for example when the import missed something or you now use a different Simkl account. Nothing is unmarked: movies and episodes Emby already has as played only get their watch date refreshed from Simkl.
